About MB-Pay

MB-Pay Premium is a web-based rewards platform created to provide a straightforward interface for users to manage MB Coins and review their account activity.

What can users do?

  • View their MB Coin balance.
  • Claim eligible in-app rewards.
  • Review reward history.
  • Manage basic profile information.
  • Submit a withdrawal request when the feature is officially enabled.

How MB Coins work

MB Coins are platform rewards. They should not be represented as guaranteed cash earnings. Any future payout program will be subject to separate eligibility rules, available balance and manual verification.

Privacy Policy

MB-Pay respects user privacy. This page explains the general information practices of the website.

Information we may store

The current demo version may store basic settings, reward history and profile information in the browser's local storage. This information remains on the user's device unless the user clears their browser data.

Advertising

MB-Pay may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense. Those services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Security

The current browser-only version should not be used to store sensitive financial information. A secure server and database are required before real-money transactions are introduced.
